Looking Ahead to Cheltenham 2025!

Join now

Kopek Des Bordes scintillating performance at the DRF means he’s now flown to the top of the market for the Supreme now heading the well regarded Salvator Mundi, however Willie Mullins bingo could come into effect where he sends his top novices this year. Kopek Des Bordes blew everyone away with the way he won in Ireland and sure looks a superstar in the making and likely to be sent off favourite wherever he goes.

Fact To File was a great winner of the Brown Advisory last year, tipped to all Pro Sports Advice members and people suspected another Gold Cup contender for Willie Mullins. He won the John Durkan in great style getting the better of Galopin Des Champs. However the 2 have gone head to head twice since in the Savills Chase and the Irish Gold Cup where Galopin Des Champs has been superior and has cemented his name as one of the best chasers we’ve seen and bids to go for his 3rd straight Gold Cup which would be some achievement. Fact To Files target remains up in the air, a lot of people believe a drop back in trip to the Ryanair is on the cards and If he was to line up in that field will have a great chance.

Gaelic Warrior is another talking point after the DRF after being well beaten by Soleness in the Dublin Chase. Gaelic Warrior was a ready winner of the Arkle last year, as our NAP of the day for all PSA members, silencing doubters who always write him off at Cheltenham as he made it 3rd time lucky as he jumped for fun and came well clear of the rest up the hill. However a few lacklustre performances since gives the impression that he could be also stepping up in trip and could go head to head with Fact to File in the Ryanair. Or will he stick to 2miles and go up against Jonbon in the Champion Chase.

The Stayers hurdle is another compelling race on the Thursday where Teahupoo looks to go back to back in the race in what looks a poor division he does look to have a strong hand this year once again. Teahupoo won well for us last year, tipped to all PSA members. He made his reappearance against Lossiemouth in not ideal conditions trip and ground wise but he still remains solid at the top of the market.

Racings biggest talking horse; Langer Dan is targeted for the Stayers hurdle after he’s won at Cheltenham the last 2 years now after being masterfully plotted by the Skeltons. Langer Dan was the highlight of the festival in 2024 for us at PSA, winning for all PSA members at 7/1 (8.00), with the Free Tip video reaching millions of people across social media. However, the handicap hasn’t taken any action this year and refuses to budge on Langer Dans mark. He could genuinely be a better horse in the Spring, however a lot of people aren’t quite convinced…

It’s still early days, but Cheltenham 2025 already looks like it could be a festival to remember. Kopek Des Bordes could be a superstar in the making, Galopin Des Champs is chasing history, and Langer Dan might just be the biggest talking horse in racing right now. Plenty of questions still to be answered, but one thing’s for sure—we’ll be right there with you, keeping an eye on the markets, the targets, and those all-important clues in the lead-up to the big week.
